Chapter one

Hello, I’m Priyanshi

I’m a UX designer in Bangalore. I got here sideways: I trained as a product designer, spent a year in the field talking to farmers and warehouse teams, and somewhere in there realised the part I loved most was understanding people.

I ask a lot of questions. I’m happiest when a problem is still a bit of a mess and nobody has decided what the answer looks like yet.

What keeps me going

Swimming early, before the pool gets busy. Long drives where the destination is an excuse. A paperback I can’t put down, finished in one sitting.

Dancing is the thing I’ve done longest and will probably never stop. And travel, mostly for the people I end up talking to.

Chapter three

Looking ahead

I want to work on things that make an actual difference in someone’s day: public services that don’t waste people’s time, tools that help a team do their job better.

I’m looking for a team that takes research seriously, argues kindly, and ships. If that sounds like yours, the next page is for you.
